About us

We've seen the same story for two decades.

Ask most children about maths in Grade 4 and they'll tell you it's their favourite subject. Homework is quick, answers are clear, and top marks come easily.

Then something changes. In the middle years, new ideas take more thinking and more time. By the senior years, the pace picks up and a single question can test several concepts at once. Even A* students start to feel overwhelmed.

After twenty years of teaching maths face to face, we've learned it isn't about ability. It's about foundations.

A banyan tree stands strong for generations because of its deep, spreading roots. Maths works the same way. Learning Banyan brings our classroom experience online, so students can build those roots early and walk into the senior years ready.

We're starting with maths, the subject we know best, with more subjects to follow.

Our approach

Maths is a marathon, not a sprint.

No one runs a marathon by training once a week. Progress comes from showing up a little every day and finishing what you start.

That's how Learning Banyan works. Short, steady daily practice builds skills that last. Refresh a concept, practise topic questions during the week, then try a full paper at the weekend.

Tips for success

Show full working

Clear steps earn marks even when the final answer slips.

Simplify early

Neat algebra makes the next step easier and cuts careless errors.

Link multi-part questions

Later parts often reuse values or ideas from earlier parts.

Mix practice modes

Short topic drills during the week, full papers at the weekend.

For parents

You've probably seen it happen.

A child who loved maths in primary school starts to struggle as the work gets harder. It isn't a lack of ability. It's gaps in the foundation.

Learning Banyan helps close those gaps early. You don't need to plan lessons or find materials. Just encourage your child to show up every day, and we'll take care of the rest.

Students in Grades 5–10 who want a strong base for senior-school maths.

How is it different from free worksheets?

Questions are hand-picked from past papers and trusted textbooks and set in a sensible order, so your child isn't practising at random.

Who is behind it?

Maths teachers with twenty years of face-to-face tutoring experience, now bringing that experience online.

A short session each day. Steady practice matters more than long hours.

Ask a question
What can I do on Learning Banyan?

You can explore courses, practise with past papers, build custom exams and question lists, view study resources, and ask about anything you're stuck on in the Question Forum.

Do I need an account for practice exams?

Yes. A free account lets you build exams, save your results and track what still needs work.

How should I revise effectively?

Refresh the concept first, then practise topic questions, then sit full papers under timed conditions. A little every day beats a long session once a week.

Where do I ask questions if I am stuck?

Post on the Question Forum's topic boards, where mentors and other students share solutions. Our resources and blogs also explain key concepts.
