Tips for success

  1. Show full working — clear steps score marks even when the final answer slips.
  2. Simplify early — neat algebra makes the next step easier and reduces careless errors.
  3. Link multi-part questions — later parts often reuse values or ideas from earlier parts.
  4. Mix practice modes — short topic drills during the week, full papers on weekends.

Frequently asked questions

What can I do on Learning Banyan?

You can explore courses, practise with past papers, build custom exams and question lists, download or view study resources, and discuss doubts on the Question Forum.

Do I need an account for practice exams?

Yes — create a free student account to build and save exams, attempt timed practice, and review your results with solutions after submission.

How should I revise effectively?

Learn topics in class or from resources first, then revise with lots of exam-style questions. Use filters for weak topics, then finish with full past papers under timed conditions.

Where do I ask questions if I am stuck?

Use the Question Forum — pick a topic board, post your question (with optional image), and get replies from the community.
